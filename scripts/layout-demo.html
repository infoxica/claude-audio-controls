How a seed becomes a tree⌄
How does a tiny seed become a tree? Explain in three short paragraphs.

A seed carries the beginning of a tree and a small store of energy. When water, warmth, and air are right, it begins to grow. Its protective shell softens, giving the tiny plant inside room to stretch.

The first root reaches into the soil, drawing in water and minerals. A small shoot then pushes toward the light. Its first leaves unfold and turn sunlight into the energy the young tree needs to grow.

Over time, the roots deepen, the trunk strengthens, and new branches form. What began as a seed becomes a living home for birds and insects, offering shade through the changing seasons.

# Privacy

The extension and userscript process native Claude speech in the browser. They do not send conversation text, audio or listening history to Infoxica or another speech provider. Claude's own requests remain subject to Claude's privacy policy.

Preferences may sync through browser extension storage. Layout and theme preferences are stored locally. Optional support reminders retain only local aggregate counters and reminder preferences; they are not transmitted and contain no conversation identity, content or audio. Audio buffers are memory-only and cleared on session replacement, navigation or tab closure. A downloaded file is saved only when requested.

The companion website loads Google Analytics only after consent and only when its dedicated measurement ID is configured. Declining leaves analytics unloaded. Consent can be withdrawn in the website's preferences. Feedback and uninstall forms do not load analytics.

Voluntary feedback includes a selected reason, optional comment, interface language, extension version, browser family and operating-system family. No name or email is requested. Cloudflare Turnstile checks submissions. Do not submit conversations or sensitive personal information. Feedback is stored privately in this project's D1 database.

The project is independent of Anthropic.
